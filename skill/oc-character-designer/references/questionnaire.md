# Concept-guided question bank

Use these as adaptive building blocks, not a fixed survey.

## S2 body batches

### Height and proportion

- **Compact** — lower height impression and shorter vertical rhythm; often feels approachable or youthful.
- **Balanced** — neutral proportion with broad styling flexibility.
- **Tall/elongated** — longer limbs and stronger vertical rhythm; often feels elegant, distant or imposing.
- **Custom** — give centimetres/range and the intended visual impression.

Ask for head-to-body ratio only after the intended rendering style is known.

### Skeleton and soft-tissue language

Offer narrow/standard/broad shoulders; high/neutral/low waist; narrow/standard/broad pelvis; lean/soft/athletic/solid body composition. Explain that these are shape relationships, not value judgments.

### Posture

Offer upright, relaxed, inward/guarded, forward-active, low-energy or custom. Ask where the weight rests and what the hands do at neutral.

## S3 face batches

### Head and lower face

Offer round, oval, long oval, heart, square or diamond tendencies. Then choose soft/clear/angular jaw and short/neutral/long chin.

### Eyes and brows

Offer round/open, almond/neutral, narrow/controlled, upturned/assertive, downturned/gentle or tired. Explain eye size, spacing and tilt separately. Ask brow shape only after eye direction.

### Nose, mouth and ears

Guide bridge height/width, tip form, profile line, mouth width, lip balance, default corners, visible teeth and ear outline. Avoid asking every field when a concise combined description resolves them.

## S5 strong references

Ask for the default hair and costume, then explicitly record allowed and forbidden changes. A default is not a permanent identity fact.

## S8 lock

Ask the user to confirm the body summary, face summary and permanent-detail list as three separate review items. Resolve contradictions before emitting the consistency lock.
