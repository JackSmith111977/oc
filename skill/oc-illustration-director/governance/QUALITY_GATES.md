# Quality Gates

## G0 Handoff valid
- manifest and hashes pass;
- upstream schema is supported;
- root canon has body and face core;
- no unresolved blocking contradiction.

## G1 Identity contract ready
- body, face and identity details are copied read-only;
- selected strong-reference state is explicit;
- no candidate variant is presented as approved.

## G2 Direction ready
- purpose, aspect ratio and narrative moment resolved;
- acting, camera, scene and lighting are not contradictory;
- secondary characters are explicitly authorized.

## G3 Prompt ready
- root canon precedes style language;
- prompt identifies selected hair/outfit state;
- negative constraints target actual drift risks;
- no hidden Canon changes.

## G4 Generation ready
- checkpoint exists;
- reference assets are present or their absence is disclosed;
- safety and visual-age checks pass.

## G5 Release ready
- output reviewed against identity contract;
- accepted/rejected status recorded;
- continuity updated only from approved output.
