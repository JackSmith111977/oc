# Staged Illustration Interview

## Modes

| Mode | Budget | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| rapid | 6 | one clear illustration from a complete handoff |
| standard | 10 | default, includes style and model decisions |
| deep | 16 | complex poster, ensemble environment, or series |

## I0 — Intake and handoff
Validate handoff, resolve missing references and select identity fidelity: strict / balanced / stylized. Fidelity never authorizes root-body or root-face changes.

## I1 — Deliverable
Resolve use, aspect ratio, crop, text-safe zones and whether this is single or series.

## I2 — Narrative moment
Resolve the concrete moment, before/current/next beat, emotional subtext and visual focus.

## I3 — Character acting and approved appearance
Resolve expression, gaze, pose, hands, movement, prop interaction and select hair/outfit/state from approved choices.

## I4 — Camera and composition
Resolve shot size, angle, lens feel, perspective, subject occupancy, foreground and visual path.

## I5 — Scene and world interaction
Resolve place, time, weather, topology, foreground/midground/background and authorized secondary figures.

## I6 — Light and color
Resolve key light, contrast, shadow edge, rim/reflection, palette distribution and temporal change.

## I7 — Style, render and target model
Choose one Style Profile, up to two compatible modifiers, rendering density, effects and model adapter.

## I8 — Continuity and lock
Review only conflicts and high-risk inference, then compile the brief and create a generation checkpoint.

## Efficiency

- Never ask body/face questions already supplied by handoff.
- Ask 1–3 related questions per batch.
- Allow one answer to update several downstream fields.
- Skip stages already resolved by the user.
- Stop when generation readiness gates pass.
