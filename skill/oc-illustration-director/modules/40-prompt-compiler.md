# Prompt Compiler

Order:

1. task and output format;
2. root body core;
3. root face core;
4. permanent identity details;
5. selected strong reference / approved variant;
6. expression, pose and interaction;
7. camera and composition;
8. scene and props;
9. lighting and color structure;
10. style profile and effects;
11. continuity constraints;
12. negative drift constraints.

Never compress away body/face facts before style language. Do not use “same character” as a substitute for concrete anchors.
