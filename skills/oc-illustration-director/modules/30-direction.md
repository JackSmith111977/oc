# Direction Model

The director controls only current-image variables:

- deliverable and crop;
- narrative beat and emotional subtext;
- expression, gaze, gesture and movement;
- selected approved appearance state;
- camera, composition and depth;
- scene topology, props and weather;
- light direction, contrast and color structure;
- style profile and post effects.

A concrete moment is preferred over a list of nouns. Every scene must answer: what just happened, what is happening, what might happen next, and what the viewer notices first.
