# Prompt templates

## Identity prefix

Preserve exact body Root Canon, exact face Root Canon and permanent identity details. Use the selected approved hair/costume state. Do not infer new permanent features.

## Body core sheet

Landscape production sheet, neutral orthographic front/strict side/back body views at equal scale and baseline, simple non-distracting base layer, height guide, head-height guide, visible hands and feet, no perspective, clean background, reserved annotation zones.

## Head and facial breakdown

Landscape sheet with neutral front, three-quarter and strict profile heads; separate eye, brow, nose, mouth, ear and profile callouts; identical facial construction in every panel; no dramatic lighting; reserved label zones.

## Strong-reference sheet

Show default hair/costume construction plus approved variations and prohibited drift. Keep Root Canon unchanged.

## Negative constraints

changed height impression, changed skeleton, changed face shape, altered eye/nose/mouth relationship, wrong apparent age, missing permanent detail, unapproved hair or outfit, perspective in orthographic views, cropped hands or feet, inconsistent scale, baked long text, watermark, signature.
