# Body and face Root Canon

## Body core

Record apparent age, concrete height or range, head-to-body ratio, silhouette, shoulder/ribcage/waist/pelvis relationship, limb proportions, neck, hands, feet, body composition, curve language, neutral posture and center of gravity.

Measurements support three modes: `descriptive`, `approximate`, `numeric`. Numeric chest/waist/hip measurements are optional and should not be forced when they are irrelevant, unwanted or inappropriate.

## Face core

Record head and face shape, forehead, cheekbones, jaw, chin, eye geometry and spacing, brows, nose front/profile, mouth and teeth, ears, skin and strict-profile risks.

## Permanent details

Store non-negotiable identifiers, asymmetries, subtle signatures and common drift risks. Hair and clothing are not Root Canon unless a truly permanent physical feature depends on them.
